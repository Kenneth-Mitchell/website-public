Monaco

Jul 2025

Monaco is a very funny place. I don't know that I captured what makes it funny — the pedestaled city sits atop a cliff face, literally and figuratively out of reach for those who cannot make the climb. Atop the rock, it feels almost liminal — I'm somewhere serene, but that serenity is wrong in some way.

Èze

Jul 2025

Èze is probably the most beautiful place I have ever visited. This ancient city hides among mountains, like a secluded grove in a dense forest. I was struck by the views within — the interesting architecture, the growing vines, the statues; I was also struck by the views without — the surrounding mountains, the disparate homes, and the far-reaching, misty ocean.

Port Vendres

Jul 2025

As is typical for me, I found myself transfixed by the ocean. The way the water caught the sunlight gave it an almost velvet texture. The water had weight — the things floating above seemed to cut through an impossibly thick liquid, gliding effortlessly across.

Valencia

Jun 2025

Valencia is gorgeous! There is such heterogeneity in the architecture — I felt like I was in a new place with every turn of a corner. I captured the architecture a lot for this reason, focusing on forms, lines, and surfaces. I quite like this group; I think it has something interesting to say about the city and the things I found.
